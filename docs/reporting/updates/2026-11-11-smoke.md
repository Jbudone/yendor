# Progress update — the smoke fixture is reproducible

The dry-Ember CI failure was traced to a wet test target. The smoke fixture now
sets its dry and wet conditions explicitly; the water rule remains unchanged.

![Current milestone map: foundation checks pass; human controls and camera are the next gate](../images/expedition-roadmap.svg)

## What changed

The old fixture selected an arbitrary enemy. A fresh instrumented run selected
the revenant at (-5, 1, 14), standing in the rain pool, with seed 43117. A wet
actor correctly refused Ember ignition, contradicting the fixture's dry assumption.

The check now orders actors by their distinct starting positions, prefers a naturally wet
actor to exercise this regression, then establishes and asserts dry and wet states
without advancing a frame. Seed and enemy-position logs make comparisons visible.

## Evidence

- The instrumented baseline reproduced the failure.
- Fixed local smoke passed against the same naturally wet target.
- All ten local Unity tests passed; all seven enemy starting positions match
  the baseline for seed 43117. This is one seed, not a broad generation proof.
- [Full Unity CI](https://github.com/Jbudone/yendor/actions/runs/42957926313) passed at `696edb2`: validation, ten tests, Editor smoke, asset audit, Linux build, packaged-player smoke and artifact upload. Ordered seed/position signatures match local Editor, hosted Editor and packaged player.
