# First expedition playtest

Hypothesis: a fresh player identifies the relic objective and deliberately
exploits wet + Storm without verbal coaching.

For each session record build/bundle hash, seed, preset, machine, participant
alias, objective-recognition time, stalls, observed interaction and post-run
explanation. Keep clips/captures linked to that build. Ask neutral questions
once the attempt ends. Do not change the hypothesis after observing results.

Initial diagnostic trial: three independent fresh players, one run each.
Provisional gate: two demonstrate both behaviors. This small sample guides
iteration and does not establish a population-level success rate.

Outcome: not run. Record supported/unsupported/inconclusive and the next change.
Movement/camera feel and timing at measured 30/60/120 fps remain separate checks.
