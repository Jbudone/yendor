User-directed follow-up after `yen-gh7`, `yen-7a1` and `yen-kn2`.
Human playtest: not run. This replaces the old foundation checklist as the next
requested session; it does not erase unresolved controls/performance questions.

## One expedition to try

Choose one of the three cores at the entry shrine with E. Read the tradeoff:
Needle favors deliberate carbine fire; Cinder favors repeated Ember attacks;
Tempest favors close-range Storm and wet enemies. The other cores disappear for
that run. R after death/extraction replays the seed so another core can be compared.

On the way out, look for broad red brutes, tall ochre spitters and small violet
flankers. Sidestep a committed warning and try cover against a spitter. The HUD
shows hit/kill confirmation, incoming damage and ability cooldowns.

Follow amber lights to the eastern overlook. Both ramps join the main road;
the elevated cache increases maximum life and offers healing. Taking this route
is optional. Gold marks the Starheart; turquoise marks the waystone.

Taking the Starheart raises an alarm and adds three mixed hunters. Turn back,
choose the road or overlook and extract with E. The exit stays open. Death ends
the expedition; R uses the same seed and N chooses the next one.

## Useful feedback

Tell us what changed your decisions, what still felt flat, and the single most
annoying failure. If an issue occurs, F8 saves a screenshot and report; preserve the
build GUID, seed, chosen core, phase and nearby events with the observation.
A short clip helps with movement/camera or attack readability problems.

remain separate, unrun gates. Automated route walking and software-rendered
captures are not human feel or performance acceptance.
